# Veil Panel 1.2.0

More ways to style your panel, with optional shortcuts and a cleaner reply window.

- **Bottom shortcuts:** Add flashlight, camera, settings or close buttons. Adjust their size and position, and optionally fade notifications behind them.
- **More presets:** Orbit, Editorial, Slate, Air and Nocturne. Presets now use a grid, with Undo kept at the bottom.
- **Card transparency and blur:** Adjust opacity from 0–100%, with separate card blur when Liquid Glass is off.
- **Matching replies:** A compact reply window styled like your notification cards, with controls for buttons, previews, text size and spacing.
- **Cleaner settings:** A smaller home header, a dedicated appearance page and system light/dark mode by default for new installations.
- **Status-bar options:** Control visibility, icon colour and transparency, or use the wallpaper behind it.

Also fixed wallpaper visibility with custom colours, conflicting glass settings, transparency in notification stacks and blur handling for older backups.

Install **VeilPanel-v1.2.0.apk** over your current version to keep your setup.

Bottom buttons are off by default. The flashlight requests optional camera permission; Veil does not take photos or record video.

Requires Android 13 or later with separate notification and quick-settings panels. Tested on REDMAGIC 10 Pro with Android 16; compatibility with other devices may vary.
