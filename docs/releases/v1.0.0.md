# Veil Panel 1.0.0

First experimental release of Veil Panel, a standalone Android notification panel built on PanOSP.

## Features

- Grouped app notifications, expandable stacks and a collapsed silent section.
- Android-provided notification actions, replies, opening and dismissal.
- Saved-wallpaper backdrop with adjustable blur and optional glass effects.
- Minimal, Frosted, Glass and Readable appearance presets, with Undo last theme.
- Custom fonts, card styling, clock/date layout and an optional liquid-glass clock.
- Real Android status icons, with an option to scroll content beneath them or keep a wallpaper-backed strip.
- Curtain drag dismissal, an adjustable bottom handle and a launcher shortcut.
- Portable ZIP backup and restore for preferences, saved wallpaper and imported fonts.
- Original Android quick settings on right-side pulls.

## Requirements and setup

Android 13 or later, with separate native notification and quick-settings panels. Physical-device coverage is limited to REDMAGIC 10 Pro running Android 16 / REDMAGIC OS 11.0.6_EU.

Install `VeilPanel-v1.0.0.apk`, enable Accessibility, notification access and display-over-apps access, then select the original image used for your Home wallpaper. See the [setup guide](../GETTING_STARTED.md).

No root, Shizuku, persistent ADB connection, Internet permission, advertising or analytics SDK is required or included.

## Known limitations

No visible native-shade overlap is currently reported in the confirmed device configuration. Other models, firmware versions and games remain unverified; Android retains control of reserved system gestures. Combined native shades are not supported.

If scrolling content appears split at the status-bar boundary, turn off **Wallpaper & glass → Scroll beneath status icons**. Real icons remain visible over a wallpaper-backed strip. Native-edge recovery and its temporary status-area cover have independent opt-outs under Motion.

Saved wallpaper is static; it does not capture other apps or follow live wallpaper. The legacy Activity backdrop fallback can pause foreground video. Settings archives do not transfer notification content or Android permission grants.
