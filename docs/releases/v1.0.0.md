# Veil Panel 1.0.0

The first experimental release of Veil Panel, based on PanOSP. Give your notifications a different look while keeping your phone’s usual quick settings on the right.

## What you can do

- Expand grouped notifications and open the silent section when you need it.
- Open, dismiss and reply to notifications when the sending app allows it.
- Choose a panel wallpaper, add blur and try glass effects.
- Start with Minimal, Frosted, Glass or Readable, with an option to undo your last preset.
- Customize cards, fonts, icons, clock and date, including a glass clock style.
- Choose whether notifications scroll beneath the status icons.
- Drag or tap the adjustable bottom handle to close the panel.
- Save and restore your setup, including your wallpaper and imported font.
- Open Veil through a compatible launcher’s app shortcut.

## Try it

You need Android 13 or later and a phone with separate notification and quick-settings panels. This release was tested on REDMAGIC 10 Pro with Android 16 / REDMAGIC OS 11.0.6_EU. Other phones have not been confirmed.

Install `VeilPanel-v1.0.0.apk`, open Veil and enable the permissions in setup. Choose a panel wallpaper, then pull down from the left to try it. Pull from the right for your phone’s original quick settings.

You do not need root, Shizuku or a computer connection. Veil has no Internet permission, ads or analytics.

The [getting-started guide](../GETTING_STARTED.md) covers setup and everyday use. It follows the current app, so some menu names and options may differ from this first release.

## Before you install

This is an experimental release. Android’s panel can interfere with gestures, and fullscreen behaviour varies between phones and apps. A single combined notification and quick-settings panel is not supported.

If scrolling looks split near the status icons, turn off **Wallpaper & glass → Scroll beneath status icons**. Under **Motion**, you can also turn off gesture recovery or its temporary status-area cover if they cause problems.

The wallpaper is a saved image and does not follow live wallpaper movement. Choose an image and allow **Display over other apps** to avoid the older background method, which can pause a video underneath.

Save a settings backup before reinstalling. Install compatible updates over the existing app to keep your setup; backups do not restore Android permissions or notifications.
