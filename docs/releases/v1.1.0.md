# Veil Panel 1.1.0

This update makes settings easier to read and gives you more control over opening and closing the panel.

## What’s new

- Material You settings with clearer sections, search, Roboto text and light or dark appearance.
- Adjustable pull-area height and width, with a live outline while you change them.
- An optional swipe up to close after reaching the bottom of your notifications. Lift your finger, swipe up again, then release after the light vibration. Pull back to keep the panel open.
- A new Demo preset with compact glass cards and a bold clock.

## Fixes

- Removed the visible pull-area highlight after closing the panel.
- Improved text contrast over light wallpapers.
- Shows the messages supplied by conversation and inbox notifications, rather than only the latest line.
- Two-notification groups now show two stacked cards; larger groups show up to three.
- Improved pull-down handling in fullscreen apps and apps that hide only the status bar.
- Fixed settings-header clipping and Android Back navigation.

## Updating

Install `VeilPanel-v1.1.0.apk` over your current version to keep your setup. You can save a backup first under **About & backup → Backup & transfer settings**.

The new swipe-up-to-close option is off by default. Enable it under **Gestures & shortcuts**. Its vibration cue follows Android’s touch-vibration setting.

Requires Android 13 or later and separate notification and quick-settings panels. Tested on REDMAGIC 10 Pro with Android 16; other devices remain unverified.
